Tamilnadu tourist guide

Iconic Historical Temples and Heritage Sites

Tamil Nadu's temple trail winds through some of India's most extraordinary heritage sites — from Madurai's massive, brilliantly sculpted Meenakshi Amman Temple, to Thanjavur's magnificent Brihadeeswara Temple, a true masterpiece of Chola architecture, along the coast to Mahabalipuram's UNESCO-listed Shore Temple and its ancient stone monolith carvings, and finally to the sacred island town of Rameswaram, linked by the iconic Pamban Bridge and home to the revered Ramanathaswamy Temple.
